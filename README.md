What can data tell us about students’ performance?

A simple online dashboard for math teachers to understand students’ performance and the factors behind success or failure.

Field: Education

Duration: 5 Days

Tool: Looker Studio

Key Dimensions:
* Total Students – Students With Failure – Passed Students Percentage – Max Grade
* Semester 1, Semester 2, and Final Grades by Student ID
* Average Final Grades by School Type, Gender, and Location (Urban / Rural)
* Filters: Family Size, Parents Status, Father Job, Mother Job, Study Time
* Number of Past Class Failures
